# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Discuss behavior
and technical decisions in good faith; respect a request to stop an interaction.

We follow the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The maintainer, [Code-oclock](https://github.com/Code-oclock), enforces this policy and may
remove content or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private
abuse-reporting tools; do not publish private details in issues.
